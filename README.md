# The Thirteenth Dimension

A 3D third-person arena action game that runs in the browser (Three.js, no build step).

Play: https://alhjmiiy-netizen.github.io/thirteenth-dimension/

## Controls

Desktop:
- **W A S D** — move
- **J / left-click** — attack (hold for a 3-hit combo)
- **Space / Shift** — dodge (invincible; a last-instant dodge triggers slow-mo) — *on the ground*
- **F** — **FLY**: toggle flight on/off. Flight is free — no MP, no stamina, no meter
- **Space (held, in the air)** — rise · **Shift (held, in the air)** — descend
- **K / L / U** or **1 / 2 / 3** — equipped skills
- **Q** — potion
- **Tab / middle mouse** — lock on
- **Mouse drag / ← →** — camera
- **Esc / P** — pause

Phone (portrait works too — landscape is roomier):
- Left stick to move, big star button to attack (hold for combos), skill buttons on the right, drag the screen to turn the camera
- **Green wing-arrow button** — **FLY**: tap to take off, tap again to land; hold it while airborne to rise
- **Down-arrow button** (appears while flying) — descend

## Flight

The Heir's wings are always his. Toggle flight anywhere, in any stage type:

- Hover, move, attack and cast at altitude; the camera pulls back and the wings run a full flap/glide cycle
- Ground melee (knights, shields, hounds) whiffs beneath you — but casters, wisps and the Seraph can still reach you, and the Warlord will leap at campers
- Your sword only reaches what is near your own height: swoop down (descend) to strike ground foes

## Notes

- Add `?q=low` to the URL for a lighter graphics mode. An FPS watchdog also steps quality down automatically if the frame rate sags.
- On high quality the arena floor is a live mirror (Reflector); a giant rotating Thirteenth Seal with a rose at its heart hangs in the sky.
